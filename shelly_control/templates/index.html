How the rules decide

Every quarter hour, a few seconds after it starts, the first of these that applies decides:

  1. An override, while it lasts.
  2. No price for this quarter, or not all of today's prices: nothing is switched.
  3. Negative prices, if on: below zero, heat.
  4. Morning top-up, if on: inside the window and under the morning line, heat.
  5. Under the switch-on line: heat.
  6. Over the switch-off line: stop heating.
  7. Otherwise the relay keeps its state, and nothing is sent.

The lines are worked out to four decimals of a euro, as the old script did, and a price exactly on a line keeps the state. Shadow mode makes and logs the same decisions, but only an override switches the relay, and nothing switches it back afterwards. A command is only sent when the relay is not already where the rules want it, and never twice in quick succession.
