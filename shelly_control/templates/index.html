Switch the boiler off completely?

The boiler will not heat at all until the time you picked. No rule switches it on, not even a negative price, and the water cools down to room temperature.

The water may be unsafe afterwards. Legionella bacteria can grow in water that stands lukewarm for days. Breathing in their droplets, for instance in shower steam, can cause a serious lung infection. Heating the water to about 60 °C kills them. This service cannot measure the water temperature.

When the vacation ends, the boiler heats for {{ heat_up_text }} whatever the price. The water is only safe to use after the boiler has fully heated up to its safe temperature again. Check that it has before you shower or use the hot tap; if you are not sure, wait. How long that takes depends on your boiler.

How the rules decide

Every quarter hour, a few seconds after it starts, the first of these that applies decides:

  1. An override, while it lasts. A vacation is an override.
  2. The Legionella guard, if on: when the water has not been fully hot for the hours set, heat until the thermostat stops, whatever the price. It needs no price.
  3. No price for this quarter, or not all of today's prices: nothing is switched.
  4. Negative prices, if on: below zero, heat.
  5. Morning top-up, if on: inside the window and under the morning line, heat.
  6. Under the switch-on line: heat.
  7. Over the switch-off line: stop heating.
  8. Otherwise the relay keeps its state, and nothing is sent.

The lines are worked out to 0.1 ct, and a price exactly on a line keeps the state. Shadow mode makes and logs the same decisions, but only an override switches the relay, and nothing switches it back afterwards. A command is only sent when the relay is not already where the rules want it, and never twice in quick succession.
